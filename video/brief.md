# Brief — "رحلة دمعة اللبان" video

GOAL: A child-friendly film that makes the jury remember Omani frankincense and see Turki's entry as complete research on the poster's five axes.
AUDIENCE: Contest jury (teachers, parents' council) at Al Baraem School (grades 1-4), and classmates.
DELIVERABLE: MP4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 85 s (2550 frames), H.264 + AAC.
SOUND: original kids' music bed + SFX, synthesized in code. No voice-over.
NUMERALS: Arabic-Indic (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), same as the game.
FOOTAGE: none; fully animated.
