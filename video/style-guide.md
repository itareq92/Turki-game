# Style guide

LOOK: needle-felted character (fibre fuzz on silhouettes, soft fibre texture) inside a matte clay world (inner shadows top-left light, grain overlay, soft drop shadows). Same renderer as the game.
PALETTE: sky #B9D9E3→#E8F1DC, hills #A7C98E / #C3D3BA, sand #EAD6A8, bark #C9B79C, resin #F1D07A, robe #F7F4ED, text #2F5A3E on cream #FFF9EE.
ACCENT: coral #E4643F only (key word in each caption). Axis chips use the poster colours: plant #3B97AC, parts #D99A1E, use #8C5FBD, importance #D24E68, benefit #4B9650.
TYPE: Baloo Bhaijaan 2. Title 150px/800. Captions 64px/800 on cream pill. Chips 38px/800. Labels 44px/800. Line-height 1.25.
MOTION TOKENS: enter ease-out-expo (0.16,1,0.3,1) 18f; exit ease-in-expo 10f; moves/camera ease-in-out-quart; pops ease-out-back; character springs ζ=0.55; text never moves while being read.
CHARACTER: stop-motion stepping at 8 fps ("on threes") for Turki and the camel, matching the felt look; camera and world move smoothly at 30 fps.
TRANSITIONS: hard cut on the bar line. One match cut S3→S4 (push into the trunk).
MUSIC: 96 BPM (18.75 f/beat, bar = 75 f). Kalimba/marimba melody, soft pad, light shaker. Major key, gentle, no drums heavier than a soft kick.
BANNED: purple/blue gradients, glow orbs, bloom, neon, lens flares, floating decorative particles, everything fading in at once, fade-and-slide-up on every element, splitting Arabic into letters, centered-everything layouts, text moving while read, invented facts.
